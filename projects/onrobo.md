# onrobo.com — monetisation

## What this is

A short .com domain I've owned for years, formerly a robotics review site, dormant
since career demands took over. This note covers what to do with it. Confirmed still
registered to me as of 2026-08-18.

## Current state

Parked deliberately. The plan is to seed a handful of strong comparison pages now so
they have time to rank, and treat the real build as a post-career-transition project
roughly two years out. No work started yet.

## Key findings

- Robot vacuums and home robotics (Roomba, Roborock, Dreame) are a large, actively
  searched affiliate category with evergreen comparison demand.
- Search is the realistic distribution channel. A newsletter signup or landing page
  with nothing pointing at it earns nothing — content has to come first.
- Robotics does well on short-form video (X, Reels, TikTok) via curated clips, with
  no on-camera presence needed. X has an active AI/robotics community.
- Universe Today (Fraser Cain) runs ad-free on Patreon — an appealing model, but it
  rests on video presence and decades of audience building. Not replicable here.
- Domain resale value: Claude's off-the-cuff estimate was low thousands, not six
  figures. **Unverified — no comparable sales were checked.**

## Constraints

- **No fabricated content.** No AI-invented reviews, no fake hands-on testing. Honest,
  curated, journalism-style material only. This is non-negotiable and shapes everything
  below.
- **Can't buy the hardware.** Reviewing robot vacuums properly means owning them, and
  that's not realistic. This is the thing that killed the original affiliate attempt.
  The workaround is synthesising real owner reviews from Amazon and Reddit —
  paraphrasing themes rather than copying — plus spec comparisons and links out to
  third-party video reviews. Positioned as curation, not as first-hand testing.
- **Time, not motivation.** I'd enjoy the social side; the job doesn't leave room.
- Work happens in spare morning gaps and whatever downtime appears, not scheduled blocks.

## The automation angle

The part I'd actually enjoy building, and it's squarely in my wheelhouse: a pipeline
watching manufacturer pages and pricing feeds, diffing for changes, and flagging stale
comparison pages for a light editorial pass. Keeps the content honest and current
without daily manual effort. Good low-stakes Claude Code project.

## Log

### 2026-08-18

Revisited what to do with the domain. Ruled out selling early — the number that would
tempt me isn't a number a five-letter niche .com commands.

The useful clarification was separating the two failure modes of the original attempt.
It wasn't that affiliate content doesn't work; it was that reviewing hardware honestly
requires owning it, and that never fit the budget. Synthesising real owner reviews
sidesteps that without crossing into fabrication, provided the site is upfront about
what it is.

Second clarification: distribution. Newsletter and social both sounded appealing, but
both need something to point at. Comparison pages that rank are the foundation;
everything else layers on later.

Landed on seeding it now and treating the real build as a retirement project. The
automation layer is the piece I'm actually looking forward to.
